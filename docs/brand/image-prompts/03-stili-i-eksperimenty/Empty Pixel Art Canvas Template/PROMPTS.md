## 1

Pixel art, 80x160 pixels, strict grid. A completely empty canvas composed of a precise grid of small, square pixels. The grid lines are thin and dark blue (#0A0F2C), forming 80 columns and 160 rows. The background inside each cell is pure white (#FFFFFF). No other elements, no shapes, no colors, no text. This is a blank template for pixel art creation. Style: minimalist, clean, high detail, sharp pixel edges, no anti-aliasing.
