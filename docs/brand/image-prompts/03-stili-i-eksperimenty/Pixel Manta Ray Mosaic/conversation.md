## USER (1762725030)

Pixel art, 80x160 pixels, strict grid. A single manta ray, centered, swimming diagonally. The entire image is composed of small, square pixels, mimicking a mosaic tile artwork for a hammam. Background: solid deep navy blue (#0A0F2C). NO OTHER ELEMENTS WHATSOEVER. NO JELLYFISH. NO CORAL. NO BUBBLES. NO TEXT. NO GRADIENTS. ONLY FLAT COLORS. The manta ray is formed by a smooth transition from dark blue (#003366) at the edges to light cyan (#00FFFF) in the center, using only these two colors and white (#FFFFFF) for highlights and "stars". Stars are single white or very light cyan pixels scattered sparsely in the background and densely within the ray's body. Style: minimalist, ethereal, glowing, serene, high detail, sharp pixel edges, no anti-aliasing. The final image must look like a physical mosaic with distinct tiles.
