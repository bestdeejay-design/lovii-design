A 10-second magical Valentine’s video based on a real photo of a couple in a cozy restaurant at night. The man in a black coat and the woman in a red plaid dress holding a wine glass, with neon lights and red heart-shaped balloons in the background. 

**Key animation details:**  
- **Opening (0-2s):** Photo gently fades in, with soft golden particles (like loyalty points) swirling around the wine glass.  
- **Mid (2-7s):** A heart-shaped portal made of terracotta and sage green energy *pulsates* behind them, growing brighter. Neon signs in the background (like the blue "J" sign) *twinkle* with warm light. Red heart balloons *float upward* while trailing glittering dust.  
- **Climax (7-9s):** The wine glass glows amber, and *golden droplets* rise from it like bubbles. Delicate floral vines *grow* from the frame’s edges, wrapping the couple in a soft embrace.  
- **Ending (9-10s):** The portal *explodes* into a shower of terracotta and sage confetti, revealing the text "LOVII" in glowing cursive.  

**Style:** Photorealistic magical realism, cinematic lighting, 4K. Color palette: terracotta, sage green, ochre, and gold. Smooth transitions, no harsh cuts. Add subtle *sound design*: gentle chime of wine glasses, soft whisper of "LOVII," and ambient jazz music.  

**Technical notes:** 24fps, 16:9 aspect ratio, slow-motion effect on particle movements. Keep the couple’s expressions warm and intimate — focus on the *emotional connection*.
