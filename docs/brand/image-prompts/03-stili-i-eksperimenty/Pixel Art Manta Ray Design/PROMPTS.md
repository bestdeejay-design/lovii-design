## 1

Pixel art, 80x160 pixels, strict grid. A single majestic manta ray, centered, swimming diagonally from bottom-left to top-right. The entire image is composed of small, square pixels, mimicking a mosaic tile artwork for a hammam. Background: solid deep navy blue (#0A0F2C). No other sea creatures, no jellyfish, no coral, no bubbles, no text. The manta ray is formed ONLY by varying shades of blue and white pixels (light blue #7EC8E3, cyan #00FFFF, white #FFFFFF) to create a glowing, starry, ethereal effect. Stars are represented as single white or light blue pixels scattered sparsely in the background and densely within the ray's body. Style: minimalist, serene, luxurious, high detail, sharp pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, only flat colors.
